Three ways to add a recipe:

  1. Snap a photo of one of Grandma's cards or a page from Mom's cookbook. The app reads the handwriting and types it up for you to check. The photo is kept next to the typed version.
  2. Paste the text of a Facebook post, Pinterest pin or recipe website. The app pulls out just the recipe.
  3. Type it in yourself.
